# Screen "2025-3"

* Received autumn 2025
* 4 panels, lots of missing baffles
* 3-pin circular connector for power (I think we have cables for this)
* EtherCon data
* Circuit board labelled "P5.2F1DR1AS2.0" - likely 5.2 mm is the pitch
* Linsn RV802DV3 receiver (small form factor)

These are probably best harvested for tiles.
